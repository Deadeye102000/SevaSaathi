# Leave Rules — UP Government Employees (Manav Sampada eHRMS)

## Casual Leave
- Entitlement: 12 days per calendar year, credited in advance.
- Cannot be combined with earned leave or medical leave in the same application.
- Cannot exceed 5 consecutive days without special sanction from the reporting officer.
- Balance lapses at year-end; does not carry forward.

## Earned Leave
- Entitlement: accrues at 2.5 days per month of service (30 days/year), credited half-yearly.
- Can be accumulated up to 300 days.
- Requires reporting officer approval; applications over 15 days require additional sanction from the district office.

## Medical Leave
- Entitlement: half-pay leave credited at 20 days/year, convertible to full pay with medical certificate.
- Requires a medical certificate from a registered government or empanelled practitioner for leave exceeding 3 days.

## General Rules
- All leave applications must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance, except medical leave.
- Leave is deemed pending until the reporting officer approves or rejects it in the system.
- An employee may withdraw a pending application at any time before approval.
- Approved leave may be cancelled only with reporting officer consent.
